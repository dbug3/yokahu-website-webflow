Hospitality Disaster Recovery and Cashflow Challenges

Catastrophe-exposed hospitality businesses, including hotels, motels, resorts, restaurants, amusement parks, and similar operations, often face significant cashflow and liquidity pressure following major natural disasters.

Even when physical damage is limited, businesses may experience business interruption, revenue loss, reduced occupancy, cancellations, cleanup costs, engineering assessments, stock replacement expenses, and other recovery-related costs that are often funded through existing cash reserves or loans such as SBA Disaster Loans.

Following a major hurricane, hospitality businesses within 50 miles of the landfall location experience an average occupancy decline of 12.5% during the first three days after a Category 3+ storm. Following major events such as Hurricane Katrina, some hospitality businesses experienced booking reductions of up to 70% for extended periods during recovery.

Coverage Gaps in Traditional Hospitality Insurance

While traditional commercial property insurance plays a critical role in helping hospitality businesses recover from physical damage, it may not address the full financial impact of a catastrophe event. Common challenges include:

  • Significant deductible exposure, with named storm deductibles commonly ranging from 1–15%, earthquake deductibles from 10–20%, and wildfire deductibles reaching up to 15% of total insured value
  • Cashflow pressure resulting from delays in the receipt of insurance claim payments
  • Non-damage business interruption and contingent business interruption exposures that may not be covered depending on policy structure, covered peril, and physical damage requirements

Catastrophe Cashflow Insurance for Hospitality Businesses

With Catastrophe Cashflow Insurance, you select a pre-agreed payout amount and the catastrophe intensity levels that would trigger a payout. If a qualifying event occurs and those conditions are met, funds are paid within days.

Catastrophe Cashflow Insurance provides rapid access to liquidity following a qualifying catastrophe event, helping hospitality businesses address business interruption, revenue loss, recovery costs, and liquidity needs.

Key benefits include:

  • Rapid liquidity following a qualifying event
  • First-dollar coverage with no deductible
  • No claims adjustment process
  • No requirement to demonstrate physical damage

By providing fast access to liquidity following a catastrophe event, Catastrophe Cashflow Insurance helps businesses manage cashflow pressure, support recovery efforts, maintain operational resilience, and recover more quickly from periods of disruption.

Understanding Parametric Insurance

Parametric insurance provides a pre-agreed payout when a qualifying catastrophe event meets predefined intensity thresholds. Unlike traditional indemnity insurance, payouts are based on the occurrence and intensity of the event rather than the amount of physical damage sustained.

  • Rapid claims process and payouts: If the trigger is met, funds are paid within days — no adjustment, no inspection.
  • No deductibles: First-dollar coverage with no named storm, earthquake, or wildfire deductible.
  • Flexible use of funds: Use the payout for repairs, debris removal, deductible exposure, business interruption, or any other recovery-related cost.
