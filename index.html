FAQs
Common questions
What is parametric weather insurance?

Parametric insurance is a fast, transparent alternative to traditional indemnity cover. Instead of assessing physical damage, it pays out automatically when a pre-agreed trigger — such as wind speed, rainfall, or earthquake intensity — is met. This eliminates claims delays and provides liquidity exactly when it’s needed.

Who is parametric weather insurance suitable for?

Parametric weather insurance is ideal for clients in catastrophe-prone or underinsured areas. It is particularly suited to businesses where weather disruption directly impacts revenue — hospitality, agriculture, events, infrastructure and real estate.

Who is Yokahu?

Yokahu is an MGA and a Lloyd’s Coverholder, founded in 2019. We are a graduate of the Lloyd’s Lab and have been developing parametric weather insurance technology for more than five years. Our platform, cat-risk.com, connects brokers, carriers and data providers worldwide.

My client already has property damage cover — would they also benefit from parametric cover?

Almost always, yes. Parametric cover is complementary to traditional insurance — it pays out immediately when a trigger event occurs, providing liquidity for business continuity costs, even where no physical damage claim has yet been settled.

How are claims handled?

Parametric policies have transparent trigger logic and automated payouts — the client is not required to initiate a claim and there is no loss adjustment process. Yokahu’s technology facilitates the speed of claims, though the payout always comes from the carrier’s loss fund.
