Understanding Parametric Insurance

Parametric insurance provides a pre-agreed payout when a qualifying catastrophe event meets predefined intensity thresholds. Unlike traditional indemnity insurance, payouts are based on the occurrence and intensity of the event rather than the amount of physical damage sustained.

Key differences include:

  • Rapid claims process & payouts: Traditional indemnity insurance typically requires claims adjustment, damage assessment, and often on-site inspections before a claim can be paid. Parametric insurance pays based on predefined event conditions. If the trigger is met, funds are paid within days.
  • No deductibles: Traditional indemnity insurance policies commonly include named storm deductibles of 1-15%, earthquake deductibles of 10-20%, wildfire deductibles of up to 15%, and hail/severe convective storm deductibles of up to 5%. Parametric insurance provides first-dollar coverage with no deductible.
  • Flexible use of funds: Traditional insurance claim payments are generally intended to restore insured property damage. Parametric insurance provides flexible liquidity that can be used to address any financial impact resulting from a catastrophe event, including repairs, engineering assessments, cleanup and debris removal, deductible exposure, business interruption, or other recovery-related costs.
